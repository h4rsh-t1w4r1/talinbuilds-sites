# Design Principles, the Anti-Slop Checklist

Apply on every build, client or own brand. Each item is a decision,
not a suggestion.

## 1. Mood before pixels
Describe the aesthetic with a real-world reference, not adjectives.
"Dark and modern" means nothing. "The inside of a high-end watchmaker's
workshop at night" is a buildable direction. Commit to ONE direction
per site. Healthcare, education, government: light mode, always.

## 2. Color, 60/30/10
Three roles, exact hexes, no additions. Primary 60% canvas and surfaces.
Secondary 30% cards, nav, dividers. Accent 10% CTAs, active states, key
callouts ONLY. An accent on headings or body copy is decoration, and
decoration is the amateur tell. Never pure black or pure white canvas.

## 3. Typography is the quality signal
A deliberate pairing, not a default. Never Inter, Roboto, or Arial as
display. The display face comes from the brand's own world. Headings
three times heavier than body in felt contrast, H1 at 64 to 72px minimum,
body line height 1.7 to 1.8, max 65 to 70 characters per line.

## 4. Whitespace is maturity
Section padding at least 120px, at least 80px between blocks. Crowding
is the fastest way to look cheap: the brain reads easy-to-process as
trustworthy. When in doubt, add space. Dashboards may trade some space
for usability, and that is correct.

## 5. The five-part hero
Outcome headline in the customer's words. Subheadline with one specific
detail in a timeframe. ONE CTA, nothing competing. A designed background.
One real trust signal. All five, every time.

## 6. The psychology passes (mark where each is used)
Halo effect: engineer the first viewport. Cognitive load: one primary
goal per section. Peak-end rule: micro-interactions create the peaks.
Smart defaults: pre-fill with the most common choice. Goal gradient:
never start at zero. Reciprocity: give value before asking. Endowment:
let visitors build something before sign-up. Loss framing: where honest,
show the cost of inaction. Contrast effect: never show a price alone.

## 7. Motion rules
Subtle, eased, purposeful. Nothing bouncing or spinning. Reduced motion
honored. Motion guides attention and signals care, never delays
understanding.

## 8. Banned words and constructions (grep to zero before delivery)
leverage, seamless, empower, unlock, robust, actionable, data-driven,
solutions, testament, landscape, delve, elevate. Em dashes. "It's not
just X, it's Y" constructions. False ranges. Vague attributions. Generic
big-finish conclusions. Carve-out: a deliberate brand device from the
design package is craft, not a tell. The difference is intent.

## 9. Coherence read
Every visible word read end to end, one brand throughout, every image
and testimonial belonging to this business. A real example shipped with
three companies' copy stitched into one clean-looking page. Layout masks
it. Reading catches it.
