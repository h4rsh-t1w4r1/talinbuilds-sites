# TALINBUILDS Brand System (Dark Academia / Gothic / Old Money)

This governs TALINBUILDS' own site, pitch decks, and outreach materials ONLY.
Never a template for a client build: every client gets a bespoke palette and
type trio pulled from their own world.

## The world
The inside of a gothic library workshop at night. Almost black walls, aged
paper, brass fittings, and one live thing in the room: a cursor glowing
green. Old Money confidence means nothing shouts.

## Core tokens
- Canvas: #111010, the near-black. Never pure #000.
- Accent: #39FF9B, the cursor green. Ten percent maximum, ideally less.
  CTAs, active nav states, hovers, the caret, key callouts. Never headings
  or body copy. Extra neutrals from the same room: #1C1A19 cards,
  #2A2724 dividers.
- Ink: #F2EFE9 primary text, aged-paper warm. Muted labels #8A857C.

## Type trio
- Display: Fraunces, heavy weight, wide tracking, minimum H1 72px.
- Body: Newsreader (or Source Serif 4), line height 1.8, max 65 characters.
- Labels: IBM Plex Mono, letter-spaced eyebrows, timestamps, HUD lines.

## Motion, two named curves
- Ink: entrance. 600ms ease-out fade and settle, ink drying into paper.
  Section entrances and scroll reveals.
- Cursor: interaction. 120 to 180ms, near-linear, no bounce. Hovers, caret
  blink, active states. The interface responds like a terminal.
Reduced motion honored always. Particles at whisper level only. Signature
element: the asymmetric T mark, as loading mark, favicon, and settle frame.

## Voice
Plain, direct, quietly confident. Numbers that are real lead the proof.
Banned words and em dash rule apply as everywhere else.

## Standing usage rules
1. The green is rare. If a screenshot has green in more than two places,
   pull some out.
2. Dark mode here, light mode for clinics and trust-first clients. The
   brand never leaks onto a client build.
3. Fraunces headlines stay short. Old Money does not explain at length.
4. Every page the studio ships passes the anti-slop checklist in
   design-principles.md. The portfolio is the proof of the pitch.
